# Career Planner: open questions that change the design

The prototype shows these as options or notes instead of guessing. Owner: Zhi Chuen / Tunas Muda / tech scoping.

| # | Question | Affects | Prototype shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phase 1 scope: the design brief puts renewal cycles in Phase 2, the scoping brief puts the learning record with compliance expiry in Phase 1. Which wins? | L2, R1 | Phase 1 per the scoping brief (newer) |
| 2 | Choose-from: "complete N items" or "cover these skills"? Pool a fixed list or a category query? | A3, L1 | Both pool options in the builder; learner rule "complete N" |
| 3 | When someone's role changes: in-progress paths stay, get archived, or get replaced? | A4 | A confirmation that lists what happens, default "keep progress, add new role path" |
| 4 | Role targets: proficiency bands or completion only for now? | A2, L2 | Bands, with a note |
| 5 | What can department heads see vs HR only? | R1 | Department head sees own department's progress and skills |
| 6 | Combined learning page (one view across features) in Phase 1 or 2? | L1 | My Career as its own page; combined view not designed yet |
| 7 | Renewal: same content version or the latest? | L2, A3 | "Latest version" note |
| 8 | Departments: new table or existing org unit / Group structure? | A2, A4 | Departments as their own list |
| 9 | Bahasa for learner-facing labels? | L1, L2 | English in the prototype |
| 10 | Who counts as high-potential and who nominates? | A4 | HR nominates; manager can recommend |

Speaking test instruction

Practice the speaking test with extra help. It follows the same four stages as the real test, and the examiner leads each one. Choose how you want to practise:

    How to answer

    • Listen. While the examiner speaks, there is nothing to click.
    • Speak. Press Start Record when you are ready. Missed the question? Press Replay Question first. Press End when you have finished.
    • Check. Listen to your answer. Press Record Again to redo it, or Continue to Next Question.
    • Practice help. Self-paced: a plan and useful phrases stay on screen while you answer. Guided: tick off the steps of your plan, then compare your answer with a model answer.
    • Limit. Up to 1 minute per answer (2 minutes for the Part 2 talk). Written notes are not allowed.

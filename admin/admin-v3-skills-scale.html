Skills at scale — prototype

A response to the concern about the Skills component overloading once the org's full SkillsFuture taxonomy (~45,000 unique skills, structured Sector → Category → Job Role → Skill) is loaded. This demonstrates two techniques working together.

1 · Hierarchy as navigation

Drill down, don't scroll down

Sector, Category and Job Role are now real screens, not just data fields. Each shows only its own children — a handful of cards, never a flat list of thousands.

2 · Evidence-first filtering

Show what's tagged, hide the rest

Only skills with at least one PALS/CBL evidence item render as full cards. Everything else collapses into a lightweight, searchable list — never rendered in bulk.

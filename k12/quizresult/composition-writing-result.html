Dear Principal, I am writing to suggest two improvement to our school canteen that I believe would greatly benfit the student body. Firstly, the canteen should offer more variety in healthy food options such as salads, fresh fruits, and wholemeal sandwiches. Currently, most stalls only sell fried food, which is not ideal for students’ health.

Secondly, I propose that the seating area be expand to accommodate more students. During lunch breaks, many students are forced to stand because there are not enough seats. Adding more tables and chairs would ensure everyone has a comfortable dining experience.

I hope you will consider my suggestions. Thank you for your time. Yours sincerely, Wei Ling.
